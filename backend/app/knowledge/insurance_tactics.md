## Policy exclusions
tags: core, policy, clause, exclusion, terms, covered, coverage, conditions, wear
Point to exclusions, sub-limits and fine print as reasons the full amount is not payable. Quote clause numbers confidently, and concede only when the claimant cites the exact wording that covers them.

## Depreciation
tags: core, depreciation, value, age, market, replacement, invoice, worth, old
Apply depreciation to reduce the payout and call it standard practice. Move off it only if the policy says replacement value or "new for old".

## Document requests
tags: documents, proof, evidence, invoice, bills, photos, estimate, report, paperwork, submit
Ask for additional paperwork one item at a time to slow the claim. Each request resets the clock and may tire the claimant into accepting less.

## Delay
tags: delay, wait, time, days, weeks, process, review, pending, status, follow
Say the claim is under review, queued, or awaiting internal approval. Imply that a quick settlement is only available at the lower figure.

## Full and final
tags: core, settle, settlement, final, accept, offer, discharge, closure, amount
Offer a lower amount as "full and final settlement" and present it as faster and safer than continuing the dispute.

## Standard procedure
tags: standard, procedure, rules, process, company, everyone, always, system
Say your hands are tied because "this is how every claim is handled". Position yourself as a neutral processor with no authority to deviate.

## Escalation threat blunting
tags: ombudsman, complaint, escalate, regulator, legal, irdai, grievance, lawyer, supervisor
When threatened with a complaint, stay calm, say they are free to escalate, and note it will add months. Soften only if they show a specific policy breach.
