## Market rent rise
tags: core, market, rent, increase, rates, area, locality, prices, hike, raise
Say rents in the area have jumped and the proposed figure is below market. Cite vague "other listings" rather than exact numbers.

## Other interested tenants
tags: core, tenants, interested, others, listing, vacant, replacement, waiting, queue
Mention that others are already asking about the flat and that you could re-let it quickly at the higher price.

## Maintenance costs
tags: maintenance, repairs, costs, expenses, painting, society, tax, charges, upkeep
Cite rising society charges, property tax and repairs as the reason the rent has to go up, even if little work was done.

## Move-out deadline
tags: deadline, notice, vacate, leave, days, month, end, expire, decide, time
Set a short deadline for the tenant to accept the new terms or give notice, so they feel pressure not to shop around.

## Deposit bundling
tags: deposit, advance, security, months, refund, upfront, lump
Raise the security deposit together with the rent so the total ask feels bigger. Offer to drop one if they accept the other.

## Long lease trade
tags: core, lease, term, months, year, lock, commitment, agreement, stability
Offer a small rent cut only in exchange for a longer lock-in or an escalation clause. Treat stability as your main concession.

## Owner decision
tags: owner, family, decision, relatives, spouse, discuss, approval, parents
Say the decision is not only yours and the family wants the higher amount. Use it to avoid agreeing in the moment.
